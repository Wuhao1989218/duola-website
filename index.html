About

Under its cute exterior lies a mowing machine. Duola Mage (朵拉法师) is a fast-paced roguelike action game:

Multiple Characters × Hundreds of Unique Weapons × Randomized Builds = A fresh mowing experience every single run.

The roguelite ladder is the core — each layer pushes deeper and hits harder, paired with 60+ levels of progression, attribute customization, equipment crafting, and out-of-run character growth! Character development meets pure mowing satisfaction.

3D Anime Action Roguelite RPG Single Player Solo Developed
